- **License Verification**: `https://github.com/wyzowl/sfx-kit/blob/master/LICENSE` ("To the extent possible under law, Wyzowl has waived all copyright and related or neighboring rights to SFX Kit.")

### Integrated SFX Files:
1. `sfx_crash.wav` (`sounds/impacts/impact-heavy-hard.wav`)
   - **Usage**: Vehicle impact with traffic or heavy barriers.
2. `sfx_impact_light.wav` (`sounds/impacts/impact-light-firm.wav`)
   - **Usage**: Curb touches, glancing wall brushes.
3. `sfx_slam_big.wav` (`sounds/cinematic-hits/cinematic-slam-big.wav`)
   - **Usage**: Fatal high-speed wreck / vehicle destruction.
4. `sfx_boost.wav` (`sounds/whooshes/whoosh-fast-hard.wav`)
   - **Usage**: Nitrous boost activation whoosh.
5. `sfx_ui_click.wav` (`sounds/interface/click-button-firm.wav`)
   - **Usage**: UI button clicks (Drive, Garage, Retry).
6. `sfx_game_over.wav` (`sounds/notifications/notification-failure-deflate.wav`)
   - **Usage**: Defeat / Results screen transition.
7. `sfx_near_miss.wav` (`sounds/notifications/notification-success-chime.wav`)
   - **Usage**: High-speed near miss close shave with traffic / combo milestone.
